Bio

I’m a computer science undergraduate at Delhi Technological University, currently an Applied Scientist Intern at Amazon.

Most of my research is about understanding models from the inside: which components a network leans on, and how much of it a task really needs. Before that I worked on generative video and on medical imaging, and those projects still shape how I think about representations.

Outside research I do competitive programming, and I’ve competed in drone and machine learning competitions with my university team.
